THE LORE

playlists.

The soundtracks to fictional situations I have become emotionally invested in for no defensible reason.
On a more serious note: While many of these playlists have specific (and slightly insane) plotlines, I consider playlists to be emotional time capsules, and I've included some of mine here.

Premise: You have a habit of picking emotionally unavailable men, but then you realise you're part of the problem.
